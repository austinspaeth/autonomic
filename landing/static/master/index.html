Downloads over time

By day of week

Average per weekday across the selected range, with a tick marking the most recent occurrence of that day — so you can see at a glance whether last Monday beat a normal Monday.

Lifecycle mix

Everyone who has ever installed, split by where they are in the lifecycle at the end of each period. Green is still inside the free trial, gold is past it, red has hit the wall and lost historical charts.

Store reach

How many people saw the listing. Page views sit on the next card beside the installs they produced, and the impression → page view rate is a tile above.

Page views → installs

The last step of the store funnel. Impressions are on their own chart because they are an order of magnitude larger — two scales never share an axis here.

Paid conversions

Sales recorded per period. Hover a bar for the revenue that came with it — money and counts never share an axis.

Conversion rates

Both steps of the store funnel, derived per period: downloads ÷ product page views, and downloads ÷ impressions. Both are percentages, so they share one axis.

Install → paid funnel

A nested funnel: the last two cohort steps are the same installs from the downloads step, aged forward to the latest reporting day — so each step is a subset of the one above it and the drop-off is purely how much time has passed. Day 7 and day 14 are inclusive, so a cohort leaves the trial on day 8 and hits the wall on day 15.

Pressure points passed per period

How many users passed each threshold in each period, against paid conversions recorded in the same period.

Conversion rate over time

Cumulative paid conversions as a share of everyone who has passed each threshold, evaluated at the end of every period.

Install cohorts

Each row is a group of installs. Age is measured from the cohort's start to the latest reporting day. Day 7 still counts as in-trial and day 14 still counts as inside the chart window — each cohort moves on the day after.

Cohort maturity

Cohort size by install period, shaded by the furthest threshold that cohort has reached as of the latest reporting day.

Add / update a single day

Entries are keyed by date + platform, so re-entering a day overwrites it. Store reporting lags, so the date defaults to the latest day that can have data — never the current day. Everything is stored in this browser's local storage — nothing leaves your machine.

Bulk add / edit days

Build a grid for a stretch of days and fill it in one pass. Days you have already entered come back pre-filled, so this doubles as bulk editing. Paste straight from a spreadsheet — click the top-left cell you want to land on and paste; tab- or comma-separated blocks spill across and down. Enter / ↑ ↓ move down and up a column, Tab moves across. Rows that already hold data are marked • and can be deleted outright with the button at the end of the row.

Trial & wall lengths

These describe how your app behaves, so they live here rather than in the filter bar — changing one re-derives every cohort metric in the dashboard. Both limits are inclusive: with 7 and 14, a user is still in the trial on day 7 and still has full charts on day 14, leaving the trial on day 8 and hitting the wall on day 15.

Backup, import & reset

CSV columns: date,platform,downloads,impressions,pageViews,updates,sales,revenue,notes. Importing merges by date + platform, overwriting matches. A conversion-rate column is ignored — it is always derived from downloads ÷ impressions.
