# Press FAQ

*Pre-answered questions a journalist will ask, matching autonomic.care/press.*

---

**What is Autonomic, in one line?**
A private recovery app for POTS, long COVID, ME/CFS and dysautonomia that turns a
five-minute morning reading into answers: how today looks, how much you can do,
and what is actually helping.

**Who is it for?**
People living with POTS, long COVID, ME/CFS, dysautonomia and the chronic
illnesses that travel with them, and anyone trying to see whether their recovery
is actually trending the right way.

**How is it private, exactly?**
There is no account and no cloud for health data. Readings are captured, scored
and stored on the phone, and every insight is computed there. Data leaves only
when the person exports it or pastes a report into an AI themselves. The app does
send anonymous usage counts and error reports to a server, with no identifier and
no health data in them, so we can tell whether it works. The privacy policy at
autonomic.care describes exactly what is sent. (See the data-flow diagram in this
kit.)

**Is it a medical device? Does it diagnose anything?**
No. Autonomic is a personal journal and education tool. It does not diagnose,
treat, or prevent any disease, and it never tells anyone to start, stop or change
a treatment.

**How does it measure HRV without a lab?**
It captures beat-to-beat intervals from a Bluetooth chest strap, an optical band
like the Polar Verity Sense, an Apple Watch, a Garmin watch, or the phone's camera
with a fingertip over the lens. It computes the standard time- and
frequency-domain HRV metrics on the phone, with artifact correction, and grades
every reading against published thresholds and the person's own baseline. A noisy
reading is flagged, not turned into a confident number.

**How is it different from Welltory, Visible or a smartwatch?**
It is built for chronic illness rather than fitness: a POTS stand test, a pacing
budget judged on the two days after, cycle-aware baselines, and insights corrected
for chance. It works with the phone camera, an Apple Watch, a Garmin or a strap
rather than one brand of band, keeps health data on the phone, and costs $49.99 a
year, up to 72% less than Welltory or Visible. The full comparison is at
autonomic.care/pricing.

**What does it cost?**
The journal is free forever, including unlimited HRV readings and every stand
test. Autonomic Pro is $7.99 a month or $49.99 a year and adds the pacing budget,
Insights, full history, stand test results and AI reports. Every install starts
with 14 days of Pro. For anyone who can't afford it, email austin@autonomic.care
and Austin will send a free code, no questions asked.

**Who built it?**
One person: Austin Spaeth, a patient-founder who has lived with long COVID for
four-plus years and built the app during his own recovery, after specialists left
him without answers. He is a solo developer and a dad of six in South Carolina.

**What platforms, and when did it launch?**
iPhone with an Apple Watch app, and Android with Health Connect, both launched in
July 2026. Garmin watches work through the Autonomic Connect IQ app.

**Do you have user data or outcomes to share?**
No health data, by design: none of it ever leaves users' phones, so there is no
dataset of readings or outcomes to share. Anonymous counts tell us roughly how
many people use the app, but they carry no identity and no health information.
