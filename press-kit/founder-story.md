## The full version (for the press page / a feature interview)

**[Open with the moment — this is the part only you can write. A sketch to react to:]**

> [In ___, my body stopped doing the automatic things it was supposed to. Standing
> up became an event. My heart rate would ___. Doctors ___. The hardest part
> wasn't any single symptom — it was not knowing whether the changes I was making
> were working, or whether I was just having a good week.]

Every tool I reached for failed the same test. The consumer wearables guessed HRV
from the wrist and rolled it into a single "readiness" number with no thresholds
behind it — a black box I was supposed to trust. The genuinely rigorous
measurement — beat-to-beat RR intervals, artifact correction, frequency-domain
analysis — lived in lab software and cardiology offices, not in my pocket. And
almost every app wanted an account and shipped my most sensitive data to a server
I'd never see.

So I built the thing I actually needed.

Autonomic captures real beat-to-beat data — from a $30 chest strap, an Apple
Watch, or a finger over the phone's camera — and runs the same class of math the
labs use, entirely on the device. It grades every reading against published
medical thresholds instead of a proprietary score, so a good day and a warning
sign look different on the screen. And it does the one thing the others wouldn't:
**it has no backend at all.** No account, no cloud. The data lives on your phone,
you own it, and I — the developer — literally cannot see it, because there's
nowhere for it to go.

That last part isn't a privacy *policy*. It's the architecture. For people whose
health data intersects with insurance and employment anxiety, "trust us not to
sell it" was never good enough. "There's nothing to sell because we never have it"
is the only version I'd stake my own recovery on.

I'm still one person. Autonomic is a subscription because that's the whole business
model — there's no data to monetize, so the app has no reason to touch yours. It's
free to use, with a hardship path for anyone who needs it. [What I want most is
___ — your honest goal for it.]
