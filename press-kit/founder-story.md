# Founder Story: Austin, founder of Autonomic

*Adapted from Austin's own telling on autonomic.care. It's already in his voice, and
this is lightly framed for press. All true; nothing invented.*

---

## The short version (for a pitch email or an "about the founder" box)

> Autonomic was built by Austin, a long-COVID patient of more than four years. After
> specialist visits in cardiology and neurology left him with no real answers, he
> started tracking his own physiology (HRV, blood pressure, sleep, the days he
> crashed) until the patterns surfaced and he found what genuinely helped.
> Autonomic is the app he built from that: a private, on-device recovery journal
> that measures instead of guesses. He's a solo founder, a long hauler, and a dad
> of six in South Carolina.

---

## The full version (for the press page / a feature interview)

For more than four years, I've been living with long COVID. It started with blood
pressure that spiked out of nowhere, a heart that raced the moment I stood up,
brain fog that swallowed whole days, and a long list of symptoms that never quite
fit together. I saw specialist after specialist in cardiology, neurology and
beyond, and kept leaving with the same thing: no real answers.

So I started tracking it myself: HRV, blood pressure, heart rate, sleep, what I
ate, the days I crashed. Slowly, patterns surfaced. I found small things that made
life more livable, and I got a little better. I'm not healed, and I won't pretend
otherwise. But over the two years I've used my own early version of Autonomic,
I've been able to see what genuinely helps and reach a far better place than I'd
been in for a long time.

I'm not a doctor. I'm not an expert. I'm just someone on this road, in South
Carolina, raising six kids, figuring out my own recovery one reading at a time.
Autonomic is the tool I built to make sense of it. If it helps you spot your own
patterns, find your own answers, and feel a little more in control of your journey
too, then it's done exactly what I hoped.

## The product/privacy bridge (for a reporter who needs the "so what")

Because Austin built Autonomic for himself first, it works the way a patient
actually needs: a five-minute morning reading from the phone camera, an Apple
Watch, a Garmin or a chest strap becomes a daily score, a pacing budget and an
early warning, graded against published thresholds and the person's own baseline
instead of a black-box score. And it keeps health data on the device: no account,
no cloud for health data, and the only automatic traffic is anonymous counts and
error reports with no identifier. For an audience with real anxieties about who
touches their health information, that isn't a privacy *policy*; it's the
architecture.

## Facts to keep straight when telling it
- **Patient-founder, solo.** Long hauler (4+ years), dad of six, South Carolina.
- **Never promise recovery.** His own line is honest: "I'm not healed, and I won't
  pretend otherwise." Say "see what's helping," never "get better."
- **The app is a journal and education tool, not a medical device.**
- The privacy claim must stay exactly true: no account, no cloud for health data,
  health data never leaves the phone unless the user exports it. The app does send
  anonymous counts and error reports with no identifier, so never say "no server"
  or "nothing is transmitted".
