# Autonomic: Press Kit

Everything a journalist needs, in one place. This folder is the *source*: the
copy and the diagram are done; you supply a photo and a handful of screenshots,
then it becomes the `/press` page on autonomic.care that every pitch links to.

**Serves two press stories** (see `pitch-angles.md`): the **patient/health** angle
(Health Rising, The Sick Times, patient orgs) and the **privacy/tech** angle (The
Markup, 404 Media, privacy writers). Same product, different reporter.

## Your homework (small)
1. **Fill the personal specifics** in `founder-story.md` and the last quote in
   `quotes.md`. That is the one thing only you can write. Don't send with brackets in it.
2. **Set a press email** (e.g. `press@autonomic.care`) and drop it into
   `fact-sheet.md` + `press-faq.md`.
3. **Gather assets** per `assets/README.md`: 6 clean portrait screenshots + a
   founder photo (the icon and logo you already have).

## Then
Say the word and I'll scaffold the `/press` route on autonomic.care from these
files so the whole kit is a single shareable link for Thursday's outreach.

## Ground rules baked into every file
- **Never promise recovery.** Say "see what's helping," never "get better."
- **Lead with the patient, not the product.**
- **The privacy claim is literally true:** no account, no cloud, no backend,
  developer can't access user data. Say it plainly.
- Autonomic is a journal/education tool, **not a medical device.**
