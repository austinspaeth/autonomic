# Pitch Angles + ready-to-send email templates

Autonomic has **two** distinct press stories. Same product, different reporter —
don't mix them. Pick the angle that matches who you're emailing, personalize the
first line so it's obviously not a blast, and keep it short.

Every pitch links to the press page (once live) and offers a walkthrough. Never
promise recovery. Lead with the patient, not the product.

---

## Angle A — Patient / health press
**For:** Health Rising (Cort Johnson), The Sick Times, ME Association, PoTS UK,
patient-org newsletters, chronic-illness writers.
**The hook:** patient-founder + real measurement science + privacy, in a niche
served mostly by hobby apps and wrist-guess trackers.

### Template

> **Subject:** A POTS/long-COVID patient built a private, offline HRV app — worth a look?
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> [One genuine sentence showing you read their work — e.g. "Your piece on ___ was
> the first thing that made ___ make sense to me."]
>
> I'm [Austin], and I have [POTS / long COVID / dysautonomia]. During my own
> recovery I couldn't find a way to answer the only question that mattered — is
> this actually getting better? — so I built one. It's called Autonomic: it
> captures lab-grade HRV from a chest strap, an Apple Watch, or just a finger over
> the camera, grades every reading against published thresholds instead of a
> black-box score, and keeps 100% of it on the device — no account, no cloud, no
> backend.
>
> It just launched on iOS and Android, it's free to use, and it's one person, no
> VC, no data to sell. I thought it might fit your readers. Press kit here:
> [link]. Happy to give you a walkthrough or a review copy — whatever's useful.
>
> Thank you for everything you write for this community.
> [Austin] · autonomic.care

## Angle B — Privacy / tech press
**For:** The Markup, 404 Media, Verge/TechCrunch privacy desks, Wired
security/privacy, EFF-adjacent newsletters, Lifehacker/How-To Geek "private
alternatives" pieces, privacy directories, r/privacy.
**The hook:** in an era of period-tracker/Dobbs fear, Flo's FTC settlement, and AI
companies hoovering health chats, here's a health app that *architecturally
cannot* leak your data.

### Template

> **Subject:** A health app with no backend — its developer can't see your data because there isn't any
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> [One specific sentence tying to their beat — e.g. "Given your reporting on

## Rules for both

- **Personalize line one, every time.** A visibly templated pitch dies.
- **Send individually.** No CC, no BCC blast.
- **One follow-up max,** ~5–7 days later, one line ("bumping this in case it's
  useful").
- **Offer, don't demand** — a walkthrough or review copy, never "please cover us."
- **No recovery claims. No competitor trash-talk.** Receipts, not attacks.
- **Track who got which angle and when** so you don't double-pitch or forget a
  follow-up.
